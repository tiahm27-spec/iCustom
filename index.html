How iCustom Works

1

Tell us what you want

Send photos, videos and details explaining exactly what you want created.

2

Connect with a creator

A seller reviews your idea and discusses the design with you.

3

Agree on the price

Product cost, weight, shipping, taxes and service fees are calculated.

4

Watch it being made

Your creator can send photos and videos showing the progress of your custom product.

5

Get your creation

Your finished custom product is shipped directly to you or someone you choose.
